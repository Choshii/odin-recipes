Lemon Pie dessert

Lemon pie is a delicious dessert that combines a tangy lemon filling with a sweet and buttery crust.

Ingredients!

  • 1 (8 ounce) package cream cheese, room temperature
  • 1 (14 ounce) can sweetened condensed milk
  • ⅓ cup lemon juice
  • 1 (9 inch) prepared graham cracker crust
  • ½ cup whipped cream for serving

Instructions

1. Step:Mix together the cream cheese, milk and lemon juice. Mix well and spread in graham crust. Chill until set, let in the refrigerator for at least 2 hours, and top as desired.

2. Step:Serve it and enjoy!
